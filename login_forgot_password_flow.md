# RRR System User Manual: Login & Password Recovery Guide

This section explains how to log into the RRR System and recover your password if you forget it. It is written in simple, non-technical terms for day-to-day users.

---

## 1. How to Log In to the RRR System

To access your RRR System dashboard, please follow these steps:

### Step 1: Open the Login Page
* Launch your web browser and navigate to the RRR System URL. 
* The system logo and login form will be displayed on the screen.

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials
* **Email Address:** Type your registered corporate email address in the **Email Address** field.
* **Password:** Type your password in the **Password** field.
  * *Tip:* You can click the **Eye icon** (👁️) on the right side of the password field to temporarily show or hide your typed password to check for typos.

### Step 3: Sign In
* Click the **Sign In** button.
* If you have Google Sign-In set up for your organization, you can click the **Sign In with Google** button to authenticate directly with your work account.
* **Redirection:** If your credentials are correct, a success message will appear, and the system will automatically open your personalized **Dashboard** page.

---

## 2. How to Reset a Forgotten Password

If you have forgotten your password, you can reset it securely using the self-service password recovery feature.

### Step 1: Open the Password Recovery Modal
* On the login page, locate the **Forgot Password?** link underneath the Sign In button.
* Click the link to open the password recovery popup window.

### Step 2: Request a Verification Code
* In the window, type your registered email address into the **Email Address** field.
* Click the **Send Code** button.
* **Check Your Email:** A 6-digit verification security code will be sent to your inbox. 
  * *Note:* Keep the window open. The code is only valid for **10 minutes**. If you do not receive the email within a couple of minutes, check your Spam/Junk folder or click the **Resend Code** link.

### Step 3: Verification & Setting a New Password
* Once you receive the email containing your 6-digit code:
  1. **Verification Code:** Type the 6-digit code into the verification box.
  2. **New Password:** Type a new, secure password.
  3. **Confirm New Password:** Re-type your new password to ensure it matches.
     * *Tip:* Use the Eye icons to confirm you typed the new password correctly.
* Click the **Reset Password** button.

### Step 4: Access Your Account
* Once the update is successful, a confirmation message will state: *"Password reset successfully! You can now login."*
* The recovery window will close automatically. You can now use your email address and new password to log in as described in Section 1.

## 3. Post-Login Dashboard & Mandatory Workflows (SOD / EOD)

Upon successful login, you are redirected to the Dashboard. However, the system enforces a strict operational workflow to ensure attendance and task tracking.

### A. Mandatory Start of Day (SOD) Submission
* **Immediate Prompt:** Before you can access any system feature, menu, or perform any actions on the software, you must submit your **Start of Day (SOD)**. The SOD form will lock the screen automatically.
* **Verification Requirements:**
  1. **GPS Access:** You must allow the web browser to access your device's GPS/location services.
  2. **Selfie Capture:** You are required to upload/capture a selfie to confirm your attendance.
* **Task Setup:** While completing your SOD, you can create and schedule your tasks for the day. These scheduled tasks will automatically appear on your **My Tasks** page.
* **Unlock:** Once the SOD is successfully submitted, the main system interface and tools will unlock for your daily operations.

### B. Dashboard Overview & Navigation
After unlocking the dashboard, the homepage acts as an interactive command center displaying key case counts for operational overview:
* **Total Active/Pending Cases:** Displays numeric summaries of your active case files.
* **Upcoming / Due Cases:** Counters showing cases nearing their response or resolution deadlines.
* **Complaint Type Analysis:** Breakdown of active cases classified by their complaint category.
* **Interactive Redirection:** All overview counters on the dashboard are **clickable**. Clicking any card or metric will instantly redirect you to the main **Cases** page with appropriate filters pre-applied to view those specific cases.

### C. Live System Notifications
* A **Notification Bell Icon** is present in the dashboard header.
* Clicking this icon opens a drop-down panel displaying recent system alerts, task assignments, updates, and feedback.

### D. Logout (Dashboard Header)
* A **Logout** option is available directly in the dashboard **top header bar**.
* Clicking it immediately ends the current user session and redirects the user back to the login page.

### E. Sign Out (Sidebar)
* A **Sign Out** option is also present at the **bottom of the sidebar navigation menu**.
* Clicking it performs the same action as the header Logout — it clears the active session and returns the user to the login screen.
* *Note:* Both the header Logout and the sidebar Sign Out buttons achieve the same result. Use whichever is most convenient based on where you are in the system.

### F. Mandatory End of Day (EOD) Submission
* **EOD Button:** When you finish your workday, you must click the **EOD (End of Day)** button on the dashboard to close out your session.
* **Strict Blockout Rule:** Submitting the EOD is **mandatory**. If you fail to submit your EOD today, **you will be blocked from starting your day (SOD) tomorrow**. You will not be able to work on the system until this is resolved. Ensure you submit EOD at the end of every shift.

## 4. "My Cases" Page & Case Management

The **My Cases** page is the primary workspace where you can search, track, and manage all assigned user cases.

### A. Case List Dashboard Features
* **Total Cases Count:** Displays a prominent, real-time indicator of the total number of cases available.
* **Filtering Options:** Includes a comprehensive set of filters (such as case status, client type, specific dates, or assignees) so you can narrow down the list and locate specific cases quickly.
* **Add New Case Button:** Provides a quick-action button at the top to create and register a new case record in the database.

### B. Accessing the Case Study (Case Sturdy)
Clicking on any individual case in the list opens the detailed **Case Study (Case Sturdy)** screen. Here, the case information is organized into five operational tabs:

#### 1. Case Details
* **Information Displayed:** Displays core details about the case, including:
  * **Company** details
  * **Services** requested
  * **Client** profile
  * **Financial Details** (estimated refund, saved amount, etc.)
  * **Summary** of the case.
* **Editing Case:** If you need to make changes, click the **Edit Case** button. You can then update the fields and save the revised information.

#### 2. Communications
* **Logging Communication:** Use this tab to enter details of any interaction or communication regarding the case. You must specify:
  * **Date & Time** of the communication.
  * **Attached Document** (if any file or communication proof is uploaded).
  * **Summary Details** (a brief explanation of the discussion/action).
* **Recent Signals:** After submitting a communication entry, the uploaded details and logs are displayed under the **Recent Signals** list for quick reference.

#### 3. Documents
* **Uploading Documents:** Go to this tab to upload any files or official documents related to the case.
* **Attach Document Option:** Use this feature to browse and upload files from your device.
* **Document Index:** All successfully uploaded documents are logged and visible in the **Document Index** section.

#### 4. Progress Update
* **Updating Case Stage:** Select the **Current Stage** of the case to advance it.
* **Forwarding Cases:** You can forward the case to another user/specialist to handle the next step in the process.
* **Important Guidelines:**
  * **Accuracy is Mandatory:** You must fill in the update details accurately.
  * **No Going Back:** Once a case stage is marked and submitted, **you cannot move the case back to a previous stage**.
  * **Closure Options:** When marking a case as closed (Closure), additional mandatory fields will appear:
    * **Refunded Amount** (the amount refunded to the client)
    * **Saved Amount** (the savings realized)
    * **Mark Compliance As Due** checkbox/option.
* **Progress Timeline:** Once submitted, all stage changes and updates are saved and chronologically displayed in the **Progress Timeline** section.

#### 5. History
* **Activity Log:** Displays the complete audit history of the case, showing who performed what actions, status updates, and date stamps.

## 5. Creating a New Case ("New Case" Form Flow)

When you need to add a new case to the system, you will be guided through a multi-stage wizard form.

### A. Stage-by-Stage Wizard Form
* **Progressive Submission:** You must fill in the mandatory fields in the current stage to unlock and advance to the next step.

### B. Company Name Validation & Duplication Check
* **Unique Company Rule:** You cannot create multiple cases with the same company name.
* **Duplication Alert:** If you type in a company name that already exists in the system, a validation error message will display: *"Company name already exist"*, and you will be blocked from proceeding until you input a unique name.

### C. Dynamic Fields by Complaint Type
Based on the selected **Type of Complaint**, the form dynamically opens different registration and upload fields:
1. **Acknowledgment Number:** Displays a field to input the acknowledgment ID along with a dedicated document upload zone.
2. **FIR Number:** Displays an input field for the First Information Report (FIR) reference ID along with a dedicated document upload zone.
3. **Grievance Number:** Displays a field to record the grievance ticket ID along with a dedicated document upload zone.
* *Note:* Uploading the corresponding legal/registration document is required for validation in this step.

### D. Client Information & Case Summary
* **Client Information:** The user must enter all mandatory client demographic, contact, and profile details.
* **Case Summary:** A descriptive overview summary of the case is required before completion.

### E. Team Assignment Setup
* **Finalizing Setup:** At the very end of the wizard, the **Team Assignment** panel opens.
* **Assigning Owners:** You must select and assign the case to the appropriate specialist, manager, or owner who will be responsible for resolving the case.

## 6. "Records" Page & Company Data Search

The **Records** page serves as the entry point for historical company archives, external systems, and future integration modules.

### A. Navigation Menu & Interface Selection
Upon navigating to the **Records** page from the sidebar, the user is presented with three main option buttons:
1. **Data Search:** Click this button (represented by a database icon) to access the internal archived databases.
2. **Odoo:** Click this button (represented by an external link icon) to be automatically redirected to the Odoo ERP login portal in a new browser tab.
3. **MOU:** This button (represented by a document text icon) is currently disabled, reserved for future integration.

### B. "Data Search" Functionality
Clicking the **Data Search** button shifts the view to the search and data lookup interface:
* **Back Option:** Click the back arrow button next to the page title to return to the primary **Records** menu.
* **Interactive Search Bar:** A search input field at the top allows users to quickly search the database by entering a **Company Name**, **Contact Person**, **BDE**, or **Email ID**. The database filters in real time to show matching records.
* **Company Data Index Table:** Below the search bar, a horizontal-scrolling table displays full archived records, including:
  * **Date & BDE:** The recording date and the assigned Business Development Executive.
  * **Company & Contact Details:** The company name, contact person, mobile number, and email.
  * **Service Details:** The specific services requested or rendered.
  * **Financial Breakdown:** Total amount including GST, base amount excluding GST, and MOU signed amount.
  * **Work & MOU Status:** The current work status, department, and MOU status (marked in green if "Signed").
  * **Remarks:** Contextual notes and feedback.
* *Tip:* Users can search for specific company names in this view to quickly verify their services, status, and related financial parameters.

## 7. Agreement Generation & Installment Management

The **Agreement Generation** page is a dedicated workspace for compiling formatted, professional settlement agreement PDFs for cases and tracking payment schedules.

### A. Filling Agreement Details
To generate an agreement document, the user must fill in the mandatory fields in the **Agreement Details** section:
* **Agreement Dates & Parties:** The agreement date, name of the first party company, and name of the second company.
* **Client Information:** The client name (which automatically syncs to the second party signatory field), client billing address, and pincode.
* **Financial Terms:** The total settlement amount (in numeric format) and the amount in words (e.g., "Ten Thousand Only").
* **Signatories:** The authorized first party and second party signatories who will sign the agreement.

### B. Installment Configuration
For cases where the settlement amount is split into multiple payments:
* **Adding Installments:** Click the **Add Installment** button (plus icon) to add dynamic installment cards.
* **Specifying Terms:** For each card added, input the specific **Amount (₹)** and the **Due Date** for that installment.
* **Removing Installments:** If an installment card was added in error or needs to be removed, click the **Delete** (Trash) icon on the top-right corner of the respective card.

### C. PDF Generation, Preview, and Download
* **Generate Button:** Click the **Generate** button (represented by an eye icon) to compile the inputs. The system generates the agreement PDF, saves the record in the backend database, and opens a live PDF preview.
* **Interactive PDF Viewer:** The built-in PDF iframe at the bottom of the page displays a native preview of the compiled agreement.
* **Download Button:** Click the **Download** button in the preview pane to download the generated PDF to your local device.
* **Clear Form:** Click the **Clear Form** button to reset all input fields and clear the installments list.

### D. "My Generated Agreements" History Log
A history log at the bottom of the page lists all agreements generated by the user:
* **Details Displayed:** Shows the client name, party companies, settlement amount, number of installments (or "Single Payment"), agreement date, and creation date/time.
* **Reviewing Agreements:** Click on any agreement row to open the generated document in a new browser tab.
* **Deleting Records:** Click the **Delete** (Trash) icon in the Action column of the table to permanently remove the agreement's tracking record.

## 8. "My Tasks" Page & Task Management

The **My Tasks** page provides a unified Kanban-style board for tracking, updating, and managing day-to-day operational tasks.

### A. Task Sources & Visibility
* **Clicking the Page:** Clicking the **My Tasks** page item in the sidebar displays all assigned tasks on the screen.
* **Task Inclusion:** The dashboard shows both manually created tasks and tasks automatically created/scheduled during the Start of Day (SOD) check-in.
* **New Task Option:** A **New Task** button is available at the top right. This option opens a creation form enabling users to create tasks that can be either directly linked to a specific case or created as general independent tasks (not related to any case).

### B. Task Columns & Progress Stages
Tasks are categorized into three columns representing their lifecycle status:
1. **To Do:** The default stage for newly created tasks.
2. **In Progress:** Represents tasks currently underway.
3. **Completed:** Tasks that have been successfully finished.

### C. Changing Stages & Updating Task Details
* **Modifying Status:** To move a task to a different stage, a user can click on the task card to open the detail panel, where they can click on the target status button (To Do, In Progress, or Completed). Users can also drag and drop cards directly between columns on the board.
* **Progress Notes:** Inside the task detail panel, users can write and save **Progress Notes** to document updates, next steps, and specific comments.
* **Reminders:** The detail panel includes a date-time field to set active **Reminders** for the task.
* **Delete Option:** A delete (Trash) icon is present at the bottom of the task detail panel to permanently remove a task.
* **Export Tasks:** An **Export** button is available on the My Tasks page (next to the New Task button). Clicking it downloads all current tasks as an Excel spreadsheet (`.xlsx`) for offline reference or reporting purposes.

## 9. "Work Report" Page & Report History

The **Work Report** (or **My Work Report**) page serves as a comprehensive portal to audit and review daily attendance and detailed activity logs.

### A. Report History Log
The main view displays a **Report History** table listing records chronologically by date:
* **Daily Metrics:** Shows check-in times (from SOD), check-out times (from EOD), and total work duration.
* **Planned Tasks:** Summarizes the planned tasks submitted during the Start of Day (SOD) form.
* **Other Activity Counter:** Aggregates and displays the counts of specific actions performed during that day:

  * **D (Documents):** Total documents uploaded and indexed.
  * **P (Progress Updates):** Total case stage updates submitted.

### B. Detailed Activity Accordion
Clicking on any date row expands the record to reveal in-depth operations and verification logs:
* **Planned Tasks & Work Summary:** Displays details of the tasks planned at SOD and the work summary submitted at EOD.
* **GPS Coordinates & Selfie Proof:** Displays location check coordinates (latitude and longitude) with a tracking link to Google Maps, alongside the selfie photo proofs uploaded during SOD and EOD checks.
* **Activity Classification Tabs:** Details are categorized into five distinct panels:
  1. **Communications:** Logs of client calls, emails, Whatsapp chats, and meetings.
  2. **Documents:** Logs of uploaded and indexed files with direct download/view links.
  3. **Progress Updates:** Case stage updates and status transitions.
  4. **Tasks Overview:** Summarized list of tasks updated, grouped by status.
  5. **All Activity:** Chronological activity feed showing all actions logged on the system for that day.

### C. Search Filters
* **Date Filter:** Restricts report rows to a specific chosen date.
* **SOD/EOD Report Filters:** A quick button bar enables filtering by **All**, **SOD**, or **EOD** check-ins.
* **User Filter (Admin Only):** Admin users can filter the logs by selecting a specific team member to audit their work reports.

## 10. "Approvals" Page & Operational Workflows

The **Approvals** page acts as the central hub for administrative authorizations, containing three primary categories: **Tour**, **Settlement**, and **Leave**.

### A. Tour (Travel Management)
Selecting the **Tour** sub-option opens the **Travel Management** interface, which contains the following features:

#### 1. New Request
Users can submit requests for upcoming business travels:
* **Employee Information:** Auto-fills employee details such as Name, Department/Role (selected from a dropdown menu), and Contact Number.
* **Trip Details:** Input fields for Purpose of Travel (e.g., Client Meeting, Business Development, or "Other" with a specify textbox), Project/Client Name, Departure/Destination Cities (with popular city suggestions), and Trip Dates.
* **Expenses Details:** 
  * **Travel Mode:** Flight, Train, Bus, Cab/Taxi, or Owned vehicle.
  * **Distance KM:** Automatically calculated using the system's distance API once departure and destination cities are entered.
  * **Auto-calculated Fare:** If the travel mode is set to 'Owned vehicle', 'Cab / Taxi', or 'Bus', the estimated fare is automatically calculated at **₹10 per KM**. Flight and Train fares are inputted manually.
  * **Meals & Accommodation:** Select meal checkboxes (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) along with meal days/rates, and accommodation details (Hotel Name, Nightly Rates, Nights, and Booking reference).
  * **Upload Proof:** Attach and upload pre-travel document proofs.
* **Advance Required Details:** Enter Advance Requested Amount (₹), Advance Mode (e.g., NEFT Bank Transfer), and special remarks.
* **Total Cost Summary:** Dynamically displays the total estimated cost of the trip based on all input metrics.
* **Travel Request List:** All submitted travel requests are listed under the **My Submitted Travel Requests** history section at the bottom of the page.

#### 2. Reimbursement Claims
Once a trip is completed, employees can claim actual travel costs:
* **Travel Request ID Linkage:** Select an **Approved** Travel Request ID from the dropdown menu (only approved tours without active reimbursement claims are visible).
* **Actual Details:** Enter actual departure/return dates, travel mode, actual distance in KM, and actual expenses incurred (Fare, Hotel, Meals, Local conveyance, Communication, and Miscellaneous) with corresponding bill numbers.
* **Advance & Net Claims:** Shows the advance already received and automatically calculates the **Net Payable to You** (Actual Claimed minus Advance Received).
* **Bills Upload:** Employees must upload original receipts and bills as proof before submitting.

#### 3. Travel Policy
Displays the official company travel policy rules, detailing limits on class of travel, daily meal allowances, hotel rates, and guidelines for reimbursement.

#### 4. Admin View (Admin/Super Admin Only)
Allows administrators to filter travel requests or reimbursement claims by user, type, or status, and either **Approve** or **Reject** them.

---

### B. Settlement (Refund Requests & Payment Workflows)
Selecting the **Settlement** sub-option allows staff to raise refund requests for clients. It involves a multi-tier approval and processing workflow.

#### 1. Settlement Request Form
* **Case Linkage:** Select the specific client case from a searchable Case ID select menu.
* **Refund Details:** Enter the total refund amount, upload supporting documents, and specify the reason/summary for the request.
* **Payment Method Options:** Four payment types are supported:
  1. **Bank:** Enter Bank Name, Account Holder, Account Number, IFSC, Branch, and Account Type (Savings/Current).
  2. **UPI:** Enter Account Holder Name and UPI ID.
  3. **Card:** Enter Bank Name, Account Holder Name, Card Number, and Branch.
  4. **QR:** Enter Account Holder Name and UPI ID/QR Link.
* **Installments Configuration:** 
  * Users can click **Add Installment** to specify multiple payments.
  * Inputting installment amounts automatically calculates the total refund amount and locks the main Amount field to read-only.
  * Individual installments can be removed using the Trash icon on the installment card.

#### 2. Administrative Approval Workflow
* **Submission:** Once submitted, the refund request is set to `Pending Admin Approval`.
* **Admin Review:** 
  * If the Admin **Approves** the request, the status updates to `Pending Payment` (forwarded to the Accountant).
  * If the Admin **Rejects** the request, they must input a rejection reason. The status changes to `Rejected`, the reason is logged, and an email notification is dispatched to the requester. The requester must then edit and re-raise the request, resetting the status back to `Pending Review`.

#### 3. Accountant Processing & Payouts (Accountant Dashboard)
Refunds marked as `Pending Payment` are forwarded to the Accountant:
* **Managing Payouts:** The Accountant reviews the bank details and payments under their Accountant Dashboard.
* **UTR Verification:** For each payout (or individual installment), the Accountant records the Bank UTR / Transaction Hash, selects the Payment Date, and uploads the payment screenshot/proof.
* **Status Updates:** Once all installments are paid, the status changes to `Paid`.
* **Accountant Rejection:** If any payout is rejected due to issues (e.g., incorrect bank details), the Accountant logs a rejection remark explaining the reason, and the request reverts to `Rejected`.

#### 4. Submitted Requests Log
A table lists all raised refund requests showing their current status (Paid, Pending, Rejected, Pending Payment). Users can search the table by Company or Case ID and filter the list by status.

### C. Leave (Leave Management & Calendar)
Selecting the **Leave** sub-option provides access to attendance auditing and time-off requests.

#### 1. Leave Request Form
* Employees can submit time-off requests by entering the Leave Type (Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Paid Leave, or "Other" with a custom textbox), Start/End Dates, Reason for Leave, and emergency contacts.
* New requests are submitted with a `Pending Review` status.

#### 2. Leave Request Approvals (Admin/Super Admin Only)
* Admins can view all pending employee leaves and select **Approve** or **Reject** to update the request status.

#### 3. User Attendance Calendar
* **Visual Grid:** Displays a daily calendar month color-coded by attendance status:
  * **Present (Green):** When a Start of Day (SOD) check-in has been logged.
  * **Absent (Red):** Workday with no log.
  * **Leave (Purple):** Dates corresponding to an approved leave request.
  * **Off Day (Blue):** Sundays or company holidays.
  * **Scheduled (Grey):** Upcoming calendar days.
* **Admin Audits:** Admins can select any non-admin employee from a dropdown list to view and audit their calendar.
* **Export Excel:** Clicking **Export Excel** downloads a complete monthly spreadsheet (`.xlsx`) containing Date, Day, Status, Check-In, Check-Out, Duration, and EOD/SOD Work Summaries for the selected user.

## 11. "Escalation MIS Report" Page & Target Details

The **Escalation MIS Report** page is a live management dashboard offering real-time insights into active, pending, and resolved cases, combined with personal and specialist-wise target performance tracking.

### A. Access Control & Role Gating
* **Operational Exclusion:** To keep the focus strictly on active recovery, non-specialist accounts (such as Admin, Operation Head, Accountant, and Operation Admin) are automatically filtered out and excluded from the Specialist Performance lists.
* **Data Access Gating:**
  * **Super Admin & Admin:** Can view top-level overview metrics and access report tabs for all specialists, allowing them to adjust targets.
  * **Operation Head:** Can access the performance sheets of all specialists under their team (i.e., those with the "Operation Review" role).
  * **Specialists:** Can only view their personal performance tab containing their individual metrics and daily/monthly progress targets (personal scope).

### B. Header Actions & Period Filters
* **Live Refresh:** Click the manual **Refresh** button (represented by a sync cycle icon) next to the report date to update all data feeds in real time.
* **Export to Excel:** Click the green **Export Excel** button to generate a compiled Excel spreadsheet (`.xlsx`) containing three distinct tabs: "Specialist Performance", "Active Cases", and "Today's Assignments".
* **Report Period Filter:** A period filter dropdown enables filtering metrics by:
  * **All Time**
  * **Current Month** (sets date ranges from the first day to the last day of the current month)
  * **Last Month** (sets date ranges to cover the previous month)
  * **Custom Date Range** (reveals interactive date pickers to set specific start and end dates)

### C. Top-Level Summary Metrics (Admin/Super Admin Only)
When logged in as an Admin or Super Admin, the top section displays four critical overview cards:
1. **Total Active Cases:** Displays the current count and total financial amount of cases currently active in the system. Clicking this card redirects the user to the Case Master list pre-filtered to show only active cases.
2. **Pending / Overdue:** Displays the count and value of cases that have passed their target dates. Clicking this card opens the Case Master page filtered by overdue cases.
3. **Total Amount at Risk:** Displays the cumulative recovery amount linked to all active case files.
4. **Assigned Today:** Summarizes new case registrations completed today. Clicking this card navigates to the Case Master page showing today's new entries.

### D. Specialist-wise Performance Matrix
* **Target Met Meter:** Displays a color-coded target completion progress bar indicating the percentage of the monthly target met:
  * **Green:** Target completion progress is **80% or higher**.
  * **Yellow:** Target completion progress is **between 50% and 79%**.
  * **Red:** Target completion progress is **below 50%**.
* **Personal & Target Tracking Metrics:** A grid displays key metrics for the selected specialist:
  * Total cases assigned, total case amount, pending cases, pending amount, resolved cases, and amount saved.
  * Cases assigned today, today's case amount, resolved today, and today's resolved amount.
* **Target Settings & Adjustments:**
  * Authorized roles (Admin, Super Admin, Operations, Operation Head) can adjust the specialist's target by clicking the **Edit** icon next to the daily or monthly target value.
  * Setting a new **Daily Target** automatically updates the corresponding **Monthly Target** by multiplying the value by 30.
  * **Target Left:** Shows the remaining amount needed to meet the monthly target. If the goal is met or exceeded, the meter displays **Achieved! 🎉**.

### E. Today's Assignments Table
* **List View:** Shows cases assigned to the team during the current day.
* **Details Displayed:** Lists the Assignee Name (with avatar), Case ID, Company Name, Total Amount, Priority level (High, Medium, Low), and current Status (Open, Pending, Critical, Resolved).
* **Interactive Filtering:** A search box allows filtering today's assignments in real time by typing the Assignee, Case ID, or Company name.
